01 / About

An education built around the whole person

I believe education should create agency, not dependency.

I am Jacey Shalé Carrier, an education entrepreneur and emerging global policy leader focused on how learning can better cultivate human potential. Across classrooms, teacher-training programs, international exchange, and online education, I have worked with learners and educators in the United States, South Korea, China, Malaysia, Europe, and Africa.

My work connects practical teaching with a bigger question: what should education help people become? I build experiences around communication, critical thinking, self-awareness, resilience, creativity, leadership, and the confidence to navigate unfamiliar problems.

That philosophy continues through MÔË — an ecosystem combining education, leadership, entrepreneurship, mentorship, AI, wellness, and global mobility.
