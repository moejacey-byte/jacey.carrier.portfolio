03 / Leadership philosophy

The learner is not a container to fill.

The learner is a person with potential to activate.

My leadership approach is rooted in the belief that education becomes transformational when people have room to ask better questions, make meaningful choices, collaborate, reflect, and create. MÔË therefore treats mentorship, experimentation, and student agency as core infrastructure — not optional extras.
