01 / About

An education built around the whole person

I believe education should create agency, not dependency.

I am Jacey Shalé Carrier, an education entrepreneur and emerging global policy leader focused on how learning can better cultivate human potential. Across classrooms, teacher-training programs, international exchange, and online education, I have worked with learners and educators in the United States, South Korea, China, Malaysia, Europe, and Africa.

My work connects practical teaching with a bigger question: what should education help people become? I build experiences around communication, critical thinking, self-awareness, resilience, creativity, leadership, and the confidence to navigate unfamiliar problems.

02 / Teaching portfolio

Across ages, settings, and goals

Teaching is where strategy becomes human.

From one-to-one online coaching to large-group professional development, I adapt the learning environment to the people in it — not the other way around.

How I teach

Adapt the method. Keep the purpose.

My teaching changes with the learner, the culture, and the goal. In language classrooms, that can mean breaking a difficult speaking task into smaller, confidence-building steps. In educator development, it means creating space for experienced teachers to test ideas, compare practice, and leave with tools they can use immediately.

I use Universal Design for Learning, Social-Emotional Learning, inquiry, project-based learning, gamification, and reflective practice because strong instruction is not simply about delivering information. It is about designing conditions in which people can participate, make meaning, ask questions, and see themselves as capable.

Across online platforms and in-person programs, I connect English and communication to the real reason a learner is studying: a university application, a job interview, an international move, a scholarship, a presentation, or simply the desire to be heard more clearly.

06C / Book & curriculum research

MÔË Steps to Success

Re-imagining the self and the education system.

MÔË Steps to Success is an ongoing self-improvement book and curriculum research project rooted in a simple question: If you could change anything about the education system or yourself, what would it be — and what is stopping you from committing to change?

The book follows my traveling journey and explores how self-awareness, lifelong learning, reflection, and proactive mindsets can help people pursue eudaimonia and ataraxia — ideas I use in this project to describe a flourishing, content, healthy, and purposeful life.

The larger research project connects personal development to education reform. My goal is to create a book, practical resources, lesson plans, and a complete curriculum that help young adults develop the skills to become active critical thinkers, independent learners, and capable adults.
