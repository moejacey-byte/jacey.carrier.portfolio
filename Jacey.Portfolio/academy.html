01 / Vision

A school designed for the whole person

Education should prepare young people for all of life.

MÔË Academy for Leaders is a school designed to truly prepare youth for all aspects of life. At its center is a holistic learning experience that brings the personal, professional, and academic dimensions of development together, with the goal of guiding students toward prosperous, purposeful, and flourishing lives.

MÔË offers a space where students can build, create, explore, and understand what type of learner they are — while discovering that education is not limited to a classroom. Knowledge, creativity, self-awareness, and leadership can be used as tools for greatness in school, work, relationships, communities, and the wider world.

The academy will provide mentorship, academic tutoring, college-preparation opportunities, and guidance that helps young people build positive mindsets, recognize their talents, and develop the confidence to shape their own futures.

“Build. Create. Understand what type of learner you are. Know that education is not just within the classroom — and learn how it can be used for greatness.”

02 / The meaning of MÔË

Pronounced “Maui”

A name built from love, change, and creativity.

MÔË derives from three words that Jacey unapologetically uses to describe herself and the way she approaches her work: Meraki, Orenda, and Elysian. Together, they express the spirit behind the academy and the way Jacey hopes to work with young people.

01

Meraki

A Greek word associated with putting love, care, and a piece of yourself into your work.

02

Orenda

An Iroquoian term associated with the power or spiritual force to create positive change in oneself and others.

03

Elysian

A Greek-rooted word evoking something beautiful, creative, idyllic, and elevated.

These ideas embody how Jacey works with youth now and how she intends to operate in the future: with care, agency, creativity, purpose, and a belief that young people can become active authors of their own lives.

05 / Leadership philosophy

The learner is not a container to fill.

The learner is a person with potential to activate.

MÔË is rooted in the belief that young people should not simply be trained to follow instructions or reproduce information. They should be given the tools, relationships, experiences, and confidence to ask better questions, make meaningful choices, collaborate, reflect, experiment, and create.

The academy therefore treats mentorship, experimentation, literacy, creativity, student agency, and self-growth as core infrastructure. The goal is not simply academic achievement; it is the development of people who can understand themselves, navigate uncertainty, solve unfamiliar problems, and contribute something meaningful to the world.

06B / Research foundation

From philosophy to practice

The book and curriculum are the first classroom.

MÔË Academy grows from an ongoing research project exploring how re-imagining the self and the education system can help young people become more self-aware, motivated, independent, and prepared for adulthood.

MÔË Steps to Success documents the personal-development journey while the curriculum translates the research into practical learning through social-emotional learning, executive functions, Universal Design for Learning, and existential skills.
